# China Southern Air Logistics

> China Southern Air Logistics Co., Ltd. (南航物流, CSAL) is the air cargo and logistics arm of China Southern Airlines, established in December 2018 and headquartered in Guangzhou. It combines the airline's freighter fleet with passenger belly capacity into an integrated air logistics network and sells its products through the Tang (唐翼) online cargo system at cargo.csair.com.
